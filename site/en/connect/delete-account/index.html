Delete your LiteDash Connect account

You can delete it from the app: Your account → Privacy and data → Delete my account. If you no longer have the app or prefer the web, sign in below with the same account and delete it from this page.

What is deleted

Your sign-in: the email, name, photo and the Google session or password you used. The name we stored with your account is deleted too. After that you can no longer sign in with it.

What stays, with no personal data

An anonymous record that the account existed, for statistics: the sign-up date, whether you signed in with Google or with email, and the date it was deleted. The model of the screen and the LiteDash version you had linked also stay. None of it carries your name or email.

When

It's deleted right away, with no waiting period. The link between your phone and the screen is stored only on those devices: it goes away when you tap Unlink in the app or uninstall it.
